The working relationship

Clear scope. Direct collaboration.

One point of contact from the first conversation through delivery. A practical process with room for thoughtful decisions.

  1. Describe the need.

    Share the idea or existing website, what needs to happen, and any deadline. Start by email; the first step is understanding whether the project is a good fit.

  2. Agree on the work.

    Define deliverables, responsibilities, price, and timing in writing. New requirements get discussed before they become extra work.

  3. Build, review, deliver.

    Review the work against the agreed scope, check it on desktop and mobile, and make the next steps clear at handoff.
