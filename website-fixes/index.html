Before you write

Questions that usually come up.

When do I pay?

Scope and price are approved in writing before payment. Sending this inquiry is free.

What has to be true before work starts?

The outcome, access, and a backup are confirmed first. Nothing on the site changes before that.

What if the problem is bigger than one repair?

Focused Website Repair is one agreed problem, usually $350–$500 after diagnosis. Security incidents, migrations, and redesigns are scoped separately. The 10-hour block is a better fit when there is more than one item.

What happens to unused hours?

No. The work is the repair or improvement agreed in writing. No sales or conversion result is guaranteed.
